## First international trip in years

I'm on my first international vacation in years. It's a huge deal after years of immigration limbo. 

## Amtrak Rail Pass

But first, I traveled across the United States by Amtrak to New York. I rode the Coast Starlight, and then the Southwest Chief to Alburquerque. It was my first time in New Mexico. I then flew to Atlanta, spent time in Philly with friends, took the Northeast Regional to London then flew to London where I'm visiting family. It feels good to be able to do this again. If you want to know why this took so long, [I wrote about our visa and green card journey](/posts/our-visa-to-green-card-journey/).

## London Calling

From New York, I flew to London on [Norse Atlantic](https://flynorse.com/). They took over Norwegian Air's planes after they shuttered. Honestly, for half the price of a full service airline, it was great. Service was efficient, the plane was fine. New York to London is only 5 and a half hours anyway.

It's so nice to be back here. I haven't spent any real amounts of time here since the early 2010s.
