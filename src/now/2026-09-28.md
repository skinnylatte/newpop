## OASec 2026

We ran [OASec](https://oasec.org/#about), and it went really well. AI is going into critical systems faster than anyone can figure out how to secure and govern it, and the people working on that problem, in research, industry and government, are mostly doing it on their own.

So we put them in one room for a day: safety and security researchers, CISOs, policymakers and practitioners. One stage, a mix of invited keynotes and talks picked by the community, on how AI systems get built, deployed, governed, attacked and defended.

## Break AI before deploying AI

I'm teaching workshops with the [Asian AI Evaluation Institute](https://asianaieval.org) on how to evaluate and red team generative and agentic AI before it gets deployed. It's hands-on, and you don't need to know how to code. We put people from public sector, product, legal, policy, risk, security and leadership in the same groups, because you need all of them to do this well.

There's a [public workshop in Singapore](https://peatix.com/event/5138876/) on 29 September 2026, and a two-hour version at [SINCON 2026](https://www.infosec-city.com/) on 16 October 2026. We also do private workshops for teams.
